FAQ

Questions?
Fair.

Odoo Community vs Enterprise?

Community is open source with no license fee. Enterprise is licensed per user and adds features like full accounting, mobile apps, Studio, and Odoo.sh hosting. We help you choose based on the modules you actually need.

How long does it take?

A simple rollout with a few core modules usually takes 1–2 months. Heavy customization or many integrations can take 3–6 months. We lock the timeline after discovery.

Our data is in Excel. Can it move?

Yes. Master data (customers, vendors, products) and opening balances almost always move. For transaction history, we agree together on how far back you need.

Where is Odoo hosted?

Odoo Online (Odoo's SaaS), Odoo.sh, or your own server, VPS or on-premise. We walk you through the trade-offs in cost, control, and ease of upgrading.

What if something breaks after go-live?

Every package includes a support period after go-live. After that, a monthly support contract covers fixes, small tweaks, and server maintenance.
