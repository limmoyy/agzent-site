FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between Odoo Community and Enterprise?

Community is open source with no license fee. Enterprise is licensed per user and adds features such as full accounting, mobile apps, Studio, and Odoo.sh hosting. We help you choose based on the modules you actually need.

How long does an implementation take?

A simple implementation with a few core modules usually takes 1–2 months. Projects with heavy customization or many integrations can take 3–6 months. We set the exact timeline after the discovery phase.

Our data lives in Excel or other software. Can it be migrated?

Yes. Master data (customers, vendors, products) and opening balances are almost always migrated. For transaction history, we agree together on how far back you need to go.

Where is Odoo hosted?

There are three options: Odoo Online (Odoo's SaaS), Odoo.sh, or your own server, either a VPS or on-premise. We walk you through the trade-offs in cost, control, and ease of upgrading.

What if something goes wrong after go-live?

Every package includes a support period after go-live. After that, a monthly support contract covers fixes, small adjustments, and server maintenance.
